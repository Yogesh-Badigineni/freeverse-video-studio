6 Built-In Creative Video Workflows

From cinematic edits to educational lessons and kids cartoons, FreeVerse Studio includes dedicated templates and automation for every format.

🎞️

Natural Video Editing

Multi-track timeline for trimming, cutting, and arranging raw camera footage, photos, and sound effects with cinematic crossfades and 1080p/4K master export.

✨

AI Script-to-Video

Natural language prompt interpreter that plans scene breakdowns, synthesizes visual cards, applies Ken Burns motion, and synchronizes narration scripts.

🧸

Kids' Cartoons & Stories

Family-safe storybook generator featuring 2D bouncy camera animations, child-friendly dialogue, colorful graphics, and child-safe content filtering.

📚

Educational & Explainers

Document ingestion engine: drag-and-drop TXT or Markdown files to auto-extract lesson outlines, bullet cards, and educational narration.

📱

Social Media Shorts (9:16)

Vertical presets optimized for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels with 3-second hook structures, captions, and call-to-action badges.

🎵

Music Visualizers

Dynamic audio-reactive visuals, beat-timed transitions, and smart background music ducking during voiceovers for podcasts and musicians.
